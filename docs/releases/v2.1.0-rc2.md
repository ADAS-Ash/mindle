## What's new

### Word-level diff inside line chunks

The diff-on-reload review surface used to strike through and tint the *whole* changed line, which made it hard to spot the actual delta — your eye had to re-read the line to find what differed. Now each chunk runs a second-pass word diff: only the removed words get the strike-through inside the red block, and only the added words get the green underline inside the green block. Unchanged words on both sides render plain. The line-level tint stays as context.

Works inside fenced code blocks too — a `print("hello")` → `print("test")` change keeps its monospace styling and shows the change at the word level, not "this entire line is different."
